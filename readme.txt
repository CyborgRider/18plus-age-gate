CyborgRider's simple selective age gate.
Stable tag: stable
License: GPLv3 or later.
Tested up to: 7.1.5
Short Description: A highly customizable age gate for posts and pages made within your wordpress site.  Optimal for sites that don't want to age gate the whole site, but who have sensitive content on select posts or pages.
Features: 
1. Pop-up that blocks the user from accessing a post until after they click the affirmative button. 
2. Customizable text for the popup from title, to message, to the buttons themselves. 
3. Customizable selection of posts by post name, by category, or by tags. 
4. Customizable redirection; send them to a specific page already on your site, or send them to a custom URL. 
5. Customizable timetable for cookies to confirm how long until the user gets a new prompt. Measured in days. Default: 365. 
6. Further customization includes the cookie Name. Default is simply smut_verified
